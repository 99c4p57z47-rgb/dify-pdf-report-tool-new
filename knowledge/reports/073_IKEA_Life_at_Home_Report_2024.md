The world
around us
As we explore enjoyment at home, it’s crucial
to understand the broader global context.
Not only does it influence the cultural trends
we see around us, but it also trickles down
into how we experience joy at home in the
everyday. This year’s report is shaped by
key movements that impact our sense of
wellbeing, both at home and beyond.
Global uncertainty and polarisation
Unpredictable events in the world around us continue
to shape our attitudes and enjoyment of home.
Political instability, extreme weather, natural disasters,
war, accelerated technological progress, regression of
progressive attitudes, increased polarisation – all can
infiltrate our personal spaces, having a direct and
indirect impact on how we feel at home. Our homes
should ideally provide a buffer against shifts in the
world around us. However, while some people do
experience home as their sanctuary from all the noise,
for others, that feeling is either missing completely
or falling well short.
