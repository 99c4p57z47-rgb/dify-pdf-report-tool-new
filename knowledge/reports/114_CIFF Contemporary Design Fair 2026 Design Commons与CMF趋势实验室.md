## 正文

On January 16, 2026, the annual theme
launch event for the China International Furniture Fair (CIFF) "Contemporary
Design Fair" was held at the CAMERICH Beijing R&D Park in Tongzhou,
Beijing. Announcing "Design Commons" as the annual theme, it set the
annual benchmark for China's design sphere, officially heralded the commencement
of the CIFF Contemporary Design Fair, and systematically outlined CIFF
(Guangzhou)'s strategic evolution from an exceptional trade platform to a
deeply empowering industry platform.

Scheduled for March 18-21, 2026, in Halls
2.1-5.1 of Area A at the Canton Fair Complex, this Contemporary Design Fair is
a pivotal initiative in CIFF's transformation from a trade platform to an
industry enabler. Spanning 40,000 square meters, it will converge over 60
commercial design brands, more than 20 designer brands, 40 overseas design
brands and studios, and present five major special exhibitions: CMF Trend
LAB_2026, Design Field, Design Snack, Design Voyage, and the Intangible
Cultural Heritage Craftsmanship Aesthetics Exhibition.

Brands at the Contemporary Design Fair

This cross-sectional gathering of the
entire design industry chain precisely reflects the tension inherent in the
term "Chinese Design" today: on one side, a grand "going
global" narrative sustained for years; on the other, the pragmatic path
every participant must navigate—concerning how products materialize, how brands
grow, and how creativity connects with industry. Between grand visions and
practical tasks, a gap has emerged—and building bridges has become the most
pressing imperative. Anchored by the theme "Design Commons," this
Contemporary Design Fair aims to construct a supportive ecosystem for Chinese
design, encompassing design, manufacturing, and commercial realization.

Five Featured Sections Outline the Future
Landscape

The fair builds a prismatic observatory and
experimental field through five special exhibitions: CMF LAB approaches from
material and color trends to provide implementable solutions; Design United
introduces international brand perspectives as a reference for commercial
design expansion; Design Dimsum, using project practices as a thread, presents
the transformation logic from need to product; Design Export focuses on Chinese
design going global, exploring cultural narratives and commercial pathways; the
Intangible Cultural Heritage Craftsmanship Aesthetics Exhibition starts from
skill inheritance and innovation, seeking the contemporary transformation of
traditional resources. They collectively deconstruct and interpret the core
theme of "Design Commons" from different angles of industry, academia,
research, and application.

CMF
LAB

In 2026, the CMF LAB special exhibition,
curated by renowned trend research firm YANG DESIGN, enters its sixth year.
This edition will revolve around four major design trends—"Algorithmic
Nature," "Noise Reduction," "Maximum Utility," and
"Slow Sound"—transforming abstract design trends into a laboratory
for pioneering furniture and spatial design.

Structurally, it presents a new dimension.
The inaugural "2020-2025 Review of CMF Trends in China's Home Furnishing
Industry" will systematically compile and showcase a timeline of trend
evolution, core color and material research findings, and industrial conversion
cases from the past five years, forming a traceable public archive.

Design United: Miniature Nations

The 2026 Design Field special exhibition,
titled "Miniature Nations" and curated by designer Li Ximi,
orchestrates a cross-temporal dialogue of civilizations. Gathering seven
international brands—Technogym, Bulo, Elite to be, ferri1956, Gandia Blasco,
Gervasoni, Lumina, Nemo—it selects five civilizations' peak periods (Ancient
Egypt, Ancient Greece, Ancient Rome, the Viking Age, and Spain's Golden Age),
constructing their respective aesthetic and philosophical systems into five
distinct experiential spaces.

This dialogue aims to provide a reference for contemporary design. By
juxtaposing distinct civilizational logics—such as Ancient Egypt's cosmic
order, Ancient Greece's formal rationality, and the Viking concept of fate—the
exhibition establishes a diverse aesthetic genealogy. It offers profound
inspiration for examining current design: in a globalized context, design
influence often stems from a deep understanding and creative transformation of
one's own cultural lineage. "Miniature Nations" thus becomes a site
that provokes reflection, guiding viewers to reconsider the value origins and
cultural coordinates of contemporary design through the juxtaposition of
different civilizations.

Design Dimsum

"Design Dimsum," a special
exhibition platform consistently curated by Wang Hongchao, takes "Design
in Practice: Products Originating from Projects" as its theme this year,
collaborating with 12 brands including Gervasoni, Slamp, RYTE, Established
& Sons, USM, Dedar, SLIDE, Benwu Edition, Nemo Lighting, FontanaArte,
pulpo, and 《Liu Hai's Dream》. The exhibition focuses not on
final style, but on the origin of design—the "custom solutions" born
from real architectural and spatial projects to solve problems. It
systematically presents how these solutions transform from one-off project
prototypes into mass-producible, maintainable product systems. Emphasizing the
underlying engineering logic and standardization pathways, it attempts to
convert validated practical methods into shareable industry resources, echoing
the collaboration, transparency, and systematic construction advocated by
"Design Commons."

Design Export : Sailing with Roots

The Design Export special exhibition uses
"Sailing with Roots" as a thread to explore how Chinese design
constructs its own narrative in an international context. Curator Zhou Anbin,
considering the authenticity of cultural translation, the feasibility of
product realization, and the long-term nature of brand development, has
selected over 15 original brands, including Dexian, Shanfu, Kong Dezheng &
Xu Yanya's GuluLu Studio, Shijian Children's Furniture, OLOVSON, Huhu、Yingshi INSTEEL, Xuduo, Xiaokong Chengxiang, MMR, File Studio, and
Studio Ololoo, all underpinned by solid craftsmanship and clear cultural
lineage.

While stylistically diverse, these works
share a composed creative attitude: not chasing fleeting trends nor being
confined to symbolic appearances, but committed to developing a contemporary
design language rooted in local living textures and craft traditions. The
exhibition unfolds like a nautical chart, recording how these brands, carrying
their cultural roots, navigate towards broader conversational waters.

Intangible Cultural Heritage Craftsmanship
Aesthetics Exhibition: Nourishing Silently

Curated by the Guangzhou Furniture
Association, this year's Intangible Cultural Heritage Aesthetics Exhibition
takes "Nourishing Silently" as its theme, narrating around home art
spaces. It meticulously selects sixteen national-level intangible cultural
heritage crafts, including Cantonese furniture, wood carving, Canton porcelain,
and Canton embroidery, and systematically introduces the "Four Great
Embroideries" for the first time, along with porcelain lineages from
Jingdezhen, Liling to Hong Kong Canton porcelain, initiating a craft dialogue
across regions and materials.

The exhibition goes beyond static display,
using scenographic spatial creation to place objects within perceptible daily
contexts, delving into the material wisdom and emotional logic embedded in
traditional techniques. Its purpose is to provide contemporary design with an
aesthetic resource that is convertible, symbiotic, and continuously nourishing,
interpreting the Eastern life philosophy of "nourishing silently."
